# SIH Demo Plan

## 1. Earth view

Open Cesium globe.

Show the four pilot cities:
- Bengaluru
- Mumbai
- Singapore
- Netherlands pilot city

## 2. Bengaluru

Fly to a selected zone.

Show:
- terrain;
- parcel boundaries;
- buildings;
- selected tower.

## 3. Building explosion

Click building.

Animate:
- full building;
- floor stack;
- selected floor;
- selected property volume.

## 4. Validation

Run/ display:
- geometry valid;
- vertical range valid;
- parent relationship;
- overlap test;
- provenance;
- identifier uniqueness.

## 5. Underground

Toggle underground layer.

Show a synthetic or authorized example of:
- basement;
- utility corridor;
- underground volume.

Label synthetic content clearly when applicable.

## 6. Switch city

Switch to Mumbai.

Demonstrate same UI/core engine with a different adapter.

## 7. International validation

Switch to Singapore or Netherlands.

Show that the canonical model remains unchanged even though source schemas differ.

## 8. Judge takeaway

The demo should make one idea unforgettable:

> The platform identifies and validates **3D volumes of urban space**, not merely 2D plots or pretty 3D buildings.
